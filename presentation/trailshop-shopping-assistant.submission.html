2

Review changed needs

Keep the selected items, flag conflicts, clear prior confirmation and private previews.

Prove customer value before expanding.

Value hypothesis: better discovery → completed selection → higher conversion. A controlled pilot tests this against a same-task baseline, not assumed figures.

3 / Decide

Commerce sponsor

Expand on proven value and acceptable unit cost—not usage alone.

Agree gates before launch. Roll back on quality or cost breaches.

Cost control

Reconcile every billed API plus preparation cost, not just the image-call cap.

Rollback safety

Engineering can revert to ordinary shopping if quality or cost gates are missed.

Privacy first

Session windows and identity design are privacy-reviewed before any adoption metric is tracked.
